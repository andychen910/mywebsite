01 / WEATHER MODELING↗

Seattle Weather Analysis: Rain, Sun, or Snow?

I used a generalized logit model in SAS and R to examine how temperature, precipitation, and wind relate to rain, sun, or snow in Seattle. In this small dataset, precipitation was the clearest indicator of rain, while minimum temperature helped distinguish snow.

SASRStatistical Modeling

02 / FINANCIAL MODELING↗

Stock Price Analysis Using GBM & OU Model

I compared Intel, AMD, and Netflix stock data in R, estimated model parameters, and simulated Intel and AMD prices with an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process. Intel and AMD log returns had a stronger correlation (0.46) than either had with Netflix (about 0.27).
